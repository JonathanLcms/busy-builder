B Busy Builder

Let's fix the busy schedule — a free, kind AI schedule organizer for students.

Free forever No account needed Private — stays on your device Kind AI, no pressure

What it does

  • Today — dump a messy list, get a clean one-day schedule
  • This Week — a box for each weekday, organized across the week
  • Alarms — timed reminders that pop up and beep
  • Email — send your finished schedule to any email
  • Shortcuts — make your own codes (HW = Homework) to type less

The AI plans realistic times, never double-books, asks when something's unclear — and is always encouraging, never judging.

Run it yourself

1. Get the code: github.com/JonathanLcms/busy-builder
2. Put an OpenAI key in a .env file: OPENAI_API_KEY=your-key
3. Run node server.js and open http://localhost:3000

The AI features run through a small local server so the key never leaves your computer — that's why this page is a showcase, not the live app.
